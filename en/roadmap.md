## Areas for further work

The items below identify areas requiring further assessment. They are not completed tests or schedule commitments. Their order depends on better-defined inputs and the findings of preceding checks.

- Establish the final weight statement and centre of gravity.
- Assess overall structural strength and review the assumptions used.
- Complete the dynamic stability assessment.
- Investigate resistance and vessel performance, with explicit limits on the applicability of the estimates.
- Check the repeatability of computational results as the model and inputs are refined.
- Define a programme of physical testing and sea trials for a stage at which those tests would be justified.

## Grounds for revising the concept

Further checks may require changes to the concept. Listing a task here does not imply that it will produce a favourable result or that the design is mature enough for construction.

Decisions about the next stage should be based on evidence and explicit limitations. Sea trials and full-scale testing have not been confirmed as of the review date.

## Updating the public summary

New findings should be reported separately from plans: what was checked, what can be concluded and what remains open. Public descriptions retain the [disclosure boundary](publication.md).

See [Development](development.md) and [Checks](tests.md) for the current state. This documentation sets no completion dates for future stages.
