What the public set includes

  • A general description of the concept and research direction.
  • Dated summaries of project status and progress.
  • Aggregate check results with explicit limits on the conclusions.
  • Open questions and areas for further assessment, without promises of outcomes or completion dates.

This is an overview of research and design work, not a documentation package for building, operating or certifying a vessel.

What remains in the private knowledge base

Engineering know-how, CAD models, detailed calculations and raw test data remain private. They are not part of this set, and no links to private data are provided here.

Public materials contain no images, diagrams, drawings or attachments, and no detailed design parameters or manufacturing instructions.

Interpreting the public information

ECONA is presented as an independent research and design project informed by current trends and advances in technology. This positioning does not claim that every element is original or that patent clearance has been established.

The public summary does not establish a completed vessel, operational readiness or certification. Withholding technical details is also not a basis for treating unconfirmed engineering properties as proven.

See Checks for the findings and their limitations, and Further work for areas requiring further assessment.
